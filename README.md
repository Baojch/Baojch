I am a Ph.D. student at Shanghai AI Laboratory and Northwestern Polytechnical University. I received my bachelor's degree from ShanghaiTech University. My work sits at the intersection of graphics, vision, and robotics, with a focus on humanoid whole-body control, motion generation, and embodied AI.

## Research Interests

- Humanoid whole-body control and locomotion
- Physics-based motion imitation
- Robot-native motion generation
- Humanoid–environment interaction
- Embodied AI and robot learning
